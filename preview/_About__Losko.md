# About — Losko

![About](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/mh6mst0y/production/3ce1f4eb06c814df43add26285ac1ae126457b55-1680x1029.jpg?rect=69,0,1543,1029&w=1024&h=683&q=75&fit=crop&auto=format)

Our articles focus on creative projects that first and foremost surprise us ourselves. In every piece we seek to reflect our aesthetic vision and sensibility. We explore trends by bringing together the finest projects from around the world, and we curate collections of remarkable things. We talk with the makers who inspire us and publish exclusive interviews that open a window onto the world of creative people.

We write about beautiful and unusual places through the lens of photographers we have come to love, and every day we seek out compelling material to broaden horizons, refine a sense of taste, and share what we have discovered with you. Minimalism, quality and substance — these are the principles that shape our understanding of the ideal piece. That is why we value concision and avoid superfluous detail, keeping our focus firmly on what is beautiful.
